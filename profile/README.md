## 📖 Introduction
Deal Finder for Windows is an AI deal finder that shops smarter than a plain search. Tell it what you want and it gathers discounts, coupons, and price drops from major stores, using AI to rank offers by real value and filter out fake "deals." It learns from your saved searches to surface more of what you actually buy. Whether you want the best price on electronics, home goods, or clothing, this AI deal finder keeps genuine savings front and center on your PC.
